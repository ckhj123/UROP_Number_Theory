# Number Theory in Function Fields: Analytic Properties & Diophantine Solvability

> **Status:** Completed Undergraduate Research Project (UROP) | Department of Mathematics, CUHK

This repository contains the research report and mathematical write-up for my Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) project under the supervision of the Department of Mathematics.

## 📄 Project Documentation
- [📄 Read Full UROP Research Report (PDF)](urop_report.pdf)

## 📐 Key Topics & Theoretical Results
- **Function Field Analogues:** Developed arithmetic properties of polynomial rings over finite fields $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$ parallel to rational integers $\mathbb{Z}$.
- **Analytic Zeta Functions:** Evaluated analytic continuations and the Euler Product Formula to derive the Prime Polynomial Theorem with explicit error terms.
- **Diophantine Equations:** Analyzed characteristic-dependent variations of the Lifting the Exponent (LTE) lemma and examined polynomial variants of Fermat's Last Theorem, Catalan's Equation ($X^m - Y^n = 1$), and the ABC theorem.
