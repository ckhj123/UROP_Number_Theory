# Number Theory in Function Fields: Analytic Properties & Diophantine Solvability

This repository contains the research report and mathematical write-up for my Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) project in the Department of Mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

##  Project Context & Team
- **Program:** Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP), Department of Mathematics, CUHK
- **Supervisor:** Dr. Ng Ming Ho
- **Team Members:** Chan Kwan Ho Jason, Tse Yan Wing Alvina, Wong Hoi Cheung Jason
- **My Primary Contributions:** Investigated analytic properties of the rational Zeta function, derived the Prime Polynomial Theorem with explicit error bounds, and analyzed characteristic-dependent variations of the Lifting the Exponent (LTE) lemma.

##  Key Topics & Theoretical Results
- **Function Field Analogues:** Developed arithmetic properties of polynomial rings over finite fields $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$ parallel to rational integers $\mathbb{Z}$.
- **Analytic Zeta Functions:** Evaluated analytic continuations and the Euler Product Formula to derive the Prime Polynomial Theorem with explicit error terms.
- **Diophantine Equations:** Analyzed characteristic-dependent variations of the Lifting the Exponent (LTE) lemma and examined polynomial variants of Fermat's Last Theorem, Catalan's Equation ($X^m - Y^n = 1$), and the ABC theorem.
